# Store listing text (draft — edit before use)

## Short description (≤ 30 chars)

> Document your family history.

## Full description

> Family Roots is a private place to record your family's ancestry and manage
> membership in your family group.
>
> - Record ancestors and the relationships between them
> - Distinguish confirmed, proposed, unknown and disputed information — the
>   app never invents an ancestor
> - Apply to join a family and have your claim reviewed by its admins
> - Walk through the recorded history with the Ancestral Journey
>
> An account and an administrator-approved family are required. This app
> connects to the Family Roots server; an internet connection is needed.

## What to avoid claiming

The current app does **not** have: data export/import, SMTP email, evidence
file uploads, living-person masking, a corrections UI, offline mode, push
notifications, or automatic backups. Do not advertise these.

## Review notes

- Apple may reject an app that is only a web view. If that happens, either add
  native functionality (push notifications, offline caching, biometric
  unlock) or distribute as a PWA instead of an App Store listing.
- Google Play asks for a Data Safety form. The app collects account details
  and family/ancestry data on the server; it does not sell data or use it for
  ads.
