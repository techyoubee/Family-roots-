## Who we are

Family Roots is operated by [YOUR NAME / ORGANISATION], contact
[SUPPORT EMAIL]. This policy explains what data the mobile apps and website
store and how they are used.

## What we collect

- **Account information** — name, email address and password (stored as a
  hash), used to sign you in and verify your address.
- **Family and ancestry records** — ancestors you add, the relationships
  between them, and their status (confirmed / proposed / unknown / disputed).
- **Membership requests and decisions** — claims you submit, their review
  outcome, and an audit log of administrative actions.
- **Journey progress** — which part of the Ancestral Journey you have seen.
- **Technical logs** — request and application logs, which can include IP
  addresses and verification links. Server logs are treated as sensitive.

## What we do not collect

The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
It has no advertising SDKs and does not sell your data. There is no
push-notification or offline storage feature in the current version.

## How we use data

To provide the service: authenticate you, store the family tree, process
membership requests and keep an audit trail. Verification messages are sent by
email when a mail relay is configured.

## Where data lives

On a server at [HOSTING PROVIDER / REGION]. Data is not transferred elsewhere.
The app requires an internet connection and loads the hosted website inside
the mobile app.

## Retention and deletion

account deletion in the app; an administrator reviews the request. Backups are
