# Store assets checklist

Replace the placeholder text with your real details. Sizes change from time to
time — confirm in each store's console before generating final files.

## Both stores

- App name: **Family Roots** (check availability in both stores)
- Short description (≤ 30 characters)
- Full description (see `LISTINGS.md`)
- Privacy policy URL (must be public, no login required)
- Support email
- Icons / screenshots (sizes below)
- Age rating questionnaire answers

## Google Play

- Icon: 512 × 512 PNG, 32-bit
- Feature graphic: 1024 × 500 PNG/JPEG
- Phone screenshots: at least 2, typically 1080 × 1920
- Signed **AAB** (Android App Bundle) from Android Studio
- Data safety form must match what the app actually does

## Apple App Store

- App icon: 1024 × 1024 PNG (no alpha, no rounded corners added by you)
- Screenshots for the required device sizes (e.g. 6.9" iPhone)
- Signed **IPA** uploaded via Xcode or Transporter
- App Privacy "nutrition label" must match the privacy policy

## Reminder

The app stores account details, recorded ancestors, family membership requests
and audit logs on **your server**. It does not access contacts, photos or
location, and it has no push-notification code yet. Only claim what is true.
